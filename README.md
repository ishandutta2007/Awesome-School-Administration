# Awesome-School-Administration

## Top School Administration Ecosystem



**Curated List of SaaS Products & Open-Source GitHub Projects**  

*Focused on Student Information Systems (SIS), School ERP, Admissions, Attendance, Gradebooks, Fees & K-12/Higher-Ed Administration*  

**Last updated: September 2026**



This repository tracks notable **SaaS platforms** and **open-source projects** for **School Administration**. These systems help schools and districts manage student records, enrollment, attendance, grades, timetables, fees, parent communication, and day-to-day institutional operations.



**Examples** include Teachmint, PowerSchool, FACTS, Blackbaud K-12 / Education Management, Gradelink, Classter, OpenEduCat, Fedena, MyClassCampus, Entab CampusCare, and Alma (the category leaders).



**Open-source emphasis**: Several mature open-source school management / SIS platforms exist and are actively used by institutions worldwide. **OpenEduCat**, **Gibbon**, **Fedena** (community), **RosarioSIS**, and **openSIS** provide strong self-hosted alternatives. This section is expanded with every major relevant project found.



Contributions welcome! Open a PR to add/update entries. Keep descriptions factual and link to official sites.

## SaaS/Hosted Platforms



- **[PowerSchool](https://www.powerschool.com/)**  

  Leading K-12 student information system widely used by large districts for enrollment, scheduling, grades, state reporting, and analytics.



- **[FACTS](https://factsmgt.com/)**  

  Comprehensive private and faith-based K-12 platform covering SIS, tuition management, admissions, and family engagement.



- **[Blackbaud K-12 / Education Management](https://www.blackbaud.com/)**  

  Cloud suite for private K-12 schools spanning academics, enrollment, tuition, advancement, and school operations.



- **[Teachmint](https://www.teachmint.com/)**  

  Mobile-first school and coaching-center platform with free tier options, classroom tools, and growing administrative features.



- **[Classter](https://www.classter.com/)**  

  All-in-one SIS/LMS/CRM platform suitable for K-12 and higher education, with strong multi-campus and automation capabilities.



- **[Gradelink](https://www.gradelink.com/)**  

  User-friendly school administration system popular with smaller private and independent schools.



- **[MyClassCampus, Entab CampusCare, Alma](https://www.myclasscampus.com/)**  

  Regional and specialized school management platforms covering campus operations, academics, and parent portals.



- **[Other school administration platforms](https://www.powerschool.com/)**  

  Additional SIS and school ERP solutions serving public districts, private schools, and international institutions.



## Open-Source GitHub Projects



- **[OpenEduCat](https://openeducat.org/)**  

  Full-featured open-source education ERP (LGPL) with 70+ modules covering SIS, LMS, fees, attendance, HR, accounting, and more. Available as Community Edition for self-hosting.



- **[Gibbon](https://github.com/GibbonEdu/core)**  

  Flexible, actively maintained open-source school management platform designed for teachers, students, parents, and leaders. Strong curriculum and timetabling focus.



- **[Fedena (Project Fedena / community)](https://github.com/projectfedena/fedena)**  

  Classic open-source school management system (Ruby on Rails) that helped popularize free SIS software; community edition remains available alongside commercial offerings.



- **[RosarioSIS](https://github.com/search?q=RosarioSIS)**  

  Open-source student information system with solid support for grades, scheduling, and (in some deployments) U.S. state reporting needs.



- **[openSIS](https://github.com/search?q=openSIS)**  

  Long-standing open-source student information system used by schools seeking a free or low-cost SIS foundation.



- **[GegoK12 and similar modern stacks](https://github.com/search?q=school+management+system+OR+SIS+open+source)**  

  Newer open-source or source-available school management projects built on modern frameworks (e.g., Laravel).



- **[Odoo / ERPNext education modules](https://github.com/odoo/odoo)**  

  Education and school management modules built on popular open-source ERP platforms that some institutions adapt.



- **[Other school & campus management tools](https://github.com/search?q=school+ERP+OR+student+information+system+open+source)**  

  Community projects covering attendance, fee collection, exam management, and parent portals.



### Additional Strong Open-Source Options



- **Learning management companions**: Moodle and other open LMS platforms frequently paired with open-source SIS tools.

- **Timetabling engines**: Open-source scheduling and constraint solvers used for complex school timetables.

- **Parent & student portals**: Lightweight open-source portal projects that surface grades and attendance.

- **Fee & invoice modules**: Accounting extensions for collecting and tracking school fees.

- **Mobile apps & APIs**: Community mobile clients and REST APIs built on top of open SIS platforms.

- Reporting and analytics stacks that connect to open-source school databases.



**Frameworks for building custom systems**:  

For a complete open-source school administration stack, evaluate **OpenEduCat** (full ERP-style coverage) or **Gibbon** (strong academic and day-to-day school focus).  

**Fedena**, **RosarioSIS**, and **openSIS** remain viable for institutions that prefer their particular feature sets or existing deployments.  

Large public districts, complex state reporting, deep analytics, and enterprise support are still dominated by commercial platforms (PowerSchool, FACTS, Blackbaud, Classter, etc.).  

Many schools successfully run open-source SIS cores while integrating commercial tools for payments, communications, or specialized compliance needs.

## Disclaimer



- This is a **community-curated** list — not exhaustive and not an endorsement.

- School administration systems handle sensitive student and family data. Deployments must comply with applicable education privacy laws (FERPA, GDPR, local regulations) and implement strong access controls, audit logging, and data-protection practices.

- Open-source software can provide excellent functionality and transparency but requires competent self-hosting, security hardening, backups, and ongoing maintenance. Institutions remain responsible for the reliability and compliance of any system they operate.
